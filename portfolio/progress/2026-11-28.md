## Why this improves the portfolio

A numerical validator can prove that a case study agrees with an artifact, but
both could still move together without documenting why the artifact changed.
The provenance layer adds an explicit review boundary: the artifact bytes,
generating commit and historical source hashes must all agree.

This demonstrates reproducible research governance, cryptographic integrity and
Git-based auditability. It does not rerun the original experiments or create
new financial, forecasting or profitability evidence.
