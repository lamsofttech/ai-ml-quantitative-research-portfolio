## Evidence summarized

The convergence study contains 16 configurations: calls and puts, plain and
antithetic sampling, four path budgets and 50 independent replications per
configuration.

| Payoff | Sampling | Mean SE: 1,000 paths | Mean SE: 64,000 paths | RMSE: 1,000 paths | RMSE: 64,000 paths |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Call | Plain | 0.448147 | 0.055733 | 0.413412 | 0.053631 |
| Call | Antithetic | 0.334079 | 0.041582 | 0.376187 | 0.037176 |
| Put | Plain | 0.294824 | 0.036960 | 0.274552 | 0.030564 |
| Put | Antithetic | 0.214175 | 0.026704 | 0.191026 | 0.028768 |

A 64-fold budget increase predicts an eightfold SE reduction; each recorded
endpoint comparison is close to that rate.

The coverage grid contains 36 configurations using 4,000 paths and 200
replications. Coverage ranged from 188/200 to 196/200 (94%-98%), and all 36
two-sided 95% exact binomial intervals contained 95%. This is consistency
evidence under shared GBM/Black-Scholes assumptions, not proof of real-market
calibration.
