## Numerical result

At degree 16, maximum approximation error is 14.363078 for equally spaced
nodes and 0.036713 for Chebyshev-Lobatto nodes: about a 391-fold reduction
(99.74%). Chebyshev nodes cluster near the endpoints and control the boundary
oscillation visible in the equidistant interpolant. Their measured error falls
from degree 4 to 16 while equidistant error grows sharply.

The comparison is deliberately qualified: Chebyshev error is about 4.9% worse
at degree 4, so this is not a claim of universal improvement at every small
degree. The 401-point grid estimates rather than proves the continuous maximum.
Interior Chebyshev nodes use float64 cosine values converted exactly to Decimal
for the reference. This is deterministic simulated numerical evidence, not
forecasting or financial-performance evidence.
