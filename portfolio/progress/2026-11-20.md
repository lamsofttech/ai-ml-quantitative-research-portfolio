## Perturbation result

At degree 16, each supplied node value was changed by at most `1e-6`:

| Node design | Target | Observed output change | Observed amplification | Lebesgue-bound gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Equidistant | -0.965 | 9.330899e-4 | 933.089921 | 6.00e-72 |
| Chebyshev-Lobatto | -0.100 | 2.723812e-6 | 2.723812 | 2.43e-74 |

The constructed amplification matches the corresponding grid-estimated
Lebesgue constant to the recorded precision. This turns the earlier bound into
an observable worst-case example: the same input-error budget can create about
343 times more output change under the equidistant design. It does not claim
that typical measurement errors are adversarially aligned.
