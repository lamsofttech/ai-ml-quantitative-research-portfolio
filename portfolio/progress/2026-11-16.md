## Numerical result

Observed coverage ranged from 188/200 (94%) to 196/200 (98%). Every one of
the 36 configuration-level exact binomial intervals includes the nominal 95%
target. The interval for 188/200 is approximately 0.8975-0.9686, so the point
estimate 0.94 is too imprecise to establish undercoverage. This is evidence that
the numerical intervals are consistent with nominal coverage on this finite
simulated grid; it is not proof of universal calibration.

The largest absolute bias was 0.035305 and the largest RMSE was 0.422041.
Absolute errors have different scales across strikes/maturities. Antithetic
sampling had lower maximum RMSE and maximum absolute bias within calls and puts
in this fixed experiment, but no speed or universal variance-reduction claim is
made.
