## Numerical result

Maximum approximation error grew from 0.438357 at degree 4 to 14.363078 at
degree 16. The degree-16 maximum float64 discrepancy from the high-precision
polynomial was only 7.82e-13. The largest total errors moved toward the
endpoints (x approximately +/-0.965).

Therefore the observed failure is dominated by the high-degree polynomial and
equally spaced node design—the Runge phenomenon—not ordinary double-precision
roundoff. The 401-point grid estimates the continuous maximum; it does not prove
the exact supremum between grid points. This is deterministic simulated
numerical evidence, not forecasting or financial-performance evidence.
